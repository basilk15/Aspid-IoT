# Tunnel-X generated assets

The current Tunnel-X product mark is `tunnel-x-mark-v4.png`. Its concept and generation prompt are documented in [the product marks guide](../PRODUCT_MARKS.md). Earlier marks and hero artwork below are retained.

Both assets were generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 25 September 2026 and copied into this project. The earlier `tunnel-x-mark.png` and `tunnel-x-mark.svg` are retained as prior assets.

## `tunnel-x-emblem-v2.png`

Transparent Tunnel-X emblem used in the product page, menus, and homepage link.

**Prompt:**

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: new Tunnel-X product emblem for an AspidIoT security website, used large in the hero and small in navigation. Create a single isolated abstract X-shaped passage mark: two precise curved channel walls converging around a narrow protected route through the center, suggesting encrypted flow after device authentication. Distinctive architectural geometry, balanced silhouette, readable at favicon size. Frosted electric cyan and deep ocean blue with very restrained cool highlights, polished 3D material but simple vector-friendly edges. Perfectly transparent background with actual alpha; centered square composition and generous clear space. No wordmark, no letters, no text, no shield, no padlock, no generic circuit traces, no mockup, no square container, no watermark.

## `tunnel-x-hero.png`

Hero artwork for the product page.

**Prompt:**

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: wide hero artwork for Tunnel-X, a premium B2B IoT security product page. Scene/backdrop: deep midnight navy space with a subtle architectural horizon, no interface or dashboard. Subject: one slender luminous cyan data path traveling from a compact device-like origin toward a distant gateway-like point, threading through two large translucent curved protective structures inspired by an X-shaped corridor. Convey private, continuous movement and controlled passage, with restrained precision and depth. Style: sophisticated CGI product campaign, clean geometry, dark ocean blues and icy cyan only, realistic light falloff, sharply art directed. Composition: landscape 3:2, focal structure on right half, left side kept dark and quiet for responsive cropping. Constraints: no text, no words, no numbers, no arrows, no shield, no lock, no people, no circuit board, no UI panels, no watermark.
