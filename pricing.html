Good to know

Questions, answered.

What exactly is Secure-X?

Secure-X is the proposed bundle of Auth-X and Tunnel-X. Auth-X authenticates a device and establishes its session; Tunnel-X protects telemetry on that session. Today they are product labels for one working device-to-sink protocol; separate module interfaces are still being designed.

What counts as an active device?

A unique registered device with at least one accepted authenticated session during the billing month. Devices with no accepted session are not billed. Overage is billed monthly in arrears, with usage alerts at 80% and 100% of the allowance. Billing thresholds never interrupt an established session.

How does annual billing work?

Prepay annually for 15% off the base subscription. The base is paid upfront; additional active-device usage remains billed monthly.

When will subscriptions be available?

Production subscriptions are proposed, not currently available to purchase. The paid design-partner pilot comes first while scale testing, reliable metering, integration boundaries and operating procedures are completed.

What is included in the pilot?

The proposed $2,500 fixed-fee, eight-week engagement covers up to five concurrent ESP32-class devices, one customer-controlled sink and telemetry use case, an integration workshop, deployment steps, a test report and scheduled engineering support. It has no production uptime SLA. 50% of the pilot fee is credited toward the first annual Secure-X license if signed within 90 days after completion. The statement of work sets acceptance criteria and hardware, network and custom firmware responsibilities. A supervised evaluation of up to five lab devices may come first; there is no self-service cloud tier.

What does the customer provide?

You provide and pay for gateway infrastructure, connectivity and retained telemetry storage. Customer-hosted plans include updates and email support, with no per-message or per-GB charge. Hosted operation, custom firmware or SDK work, on-site installation and formal SLA support are quoted separately.

Can Tunnel-X be licensed on its own?

That is the proposed separate license for customers who already have a compatible identity and session provider. Tunnel-X still needs a trusted session to protect telemetry. Its external provider interface must be designed and tested before standalone production sales; integration work is scoped separately. Customers using Auth-X can upgrade to Secure-X instead.

Can we pilot Tunnel-X with our own session provider?

We can scope a focused pilot if your session provider can supply the trust and key state Tunnel-X needs. The standard $2,500 pilot covers the current integrated path; external adapter design and validation are quoted in the statement of work.
